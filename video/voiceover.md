# Voice-over script: product video (67 s)

The video ships with captions only. This script is for a voice-over recorded later.

- **Voice:** calm, warm, unhurried, about 140 words a minute.
- **Timings:** each line starts at the scene's time in `src/timeline.ts` and fits inside that scene.
- **Wording:** keep these limits if you edit it. No medical claims ("ADHD-friendly" only), no financial advice, no brand names.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 s | Can I afford this? | You're at the checkout, and the question comes up again: can I afford this? Your bank shows a balance. It doesn't show what's safe to spend. |
| 5–11 s | One number: safe to spend today. | Where It All Is gives you one number: what's safe to spend today. Bills due before payday are already set aside. |
| 11–17 s | Log a spend in seconds. | Logging a spend takes seconds. Type "twelve fifty lunch", or tap a chip. |
| 17–23 s | Bills and payday, already counted. | Your bills and your payday are on the calendar, and they're already counted in your number. |
| 23–30 s | Drop in your bank statement. | Don't want to type? Drop in your bank statement. Anything already there is skipped, so nothing is counted twice. |
| 30–37 s | Away for a week? No guilt. | Been away for a week? No guilt. It walks you back in, one question at a time. |
| 37–44 s | Envelopes, goals, a debt-free date. | When you're ready: envelopes, savings goals, and a date you could be debt-free. |
| 44–50 s | See where it all went. | And at the end of the month, see where it all went. |
| 50–56 s | Private by design. | No bank login. No account. Your data stays on your device. |
| 56–62 s | Works on everything you own. | It works on your phone, your tablet and your laptop. |
| 62–67 s | Try the free demo. | Where It All Is. An ADHD-friendly budget app, with no subscription. Try the free demo. |
